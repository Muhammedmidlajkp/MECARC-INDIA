Non-Woven Bag Making Machines

Efficiently produce durable, reusable non-woven fabric bags in various shapes and sizes.

Our non-woven bag making machines are fully automatic and designed for high-speed production of various bag types, including D-cut, U-cut, and handle bags. Utilizing ultrasonic sealing technology, they ensure strong, reliable seams for durable and reusable bags.

With a user-friendly PLC control system and computerized precision, these machines offer a cost-effective and eco-friendly alternative to traditional plastic bags, perfect for retail, promotional, and grocery applications.
